📱 Social Hub Mobile

Download and install Social Hub on your Android or iOS device

🤖 Android

Install as PWA or scan QR code

📋 Installation Steps:
  1. Open the link on your Android phone
  2. Tap ⋮ (menu) or ⋮⋮ (more)
  3. Select "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"
  4. Tap "Add" or "Install"
  5. Social Hub will appear on your home screen!
✓ Works Offline

🍎 iOS

Install as PWA or scan QR code

📋 Installation Steps:
  1. Open the link on your iPhone/iPad
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. Scroll and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Customize the name (optional)
  5. Tap "Add" in the top right
  6. Social Hub will appear on your home screen!
✓ Works Offline
